About the club

More than a gym.
A fighting family.

Northern Spirit is a Muay Thai and boxing club in Sale, Greater Manchester. We opened our doors to give people a place to train properly, whether that means competing, getting fit, or finding a community that pushes you to be better.

Our coaches have decades of combined experience in the ring and on the mats. We keep class sizes small, standards high, and egos at the door.

Welcoming

Every body and every background. We train hard and look after each other.

Community

A team, not a gym membership. You'll know names and make friends.

Standards

Proper coaching, real technique, and a culture that demands your best.

Grit

Show up consistently. The work speaks for itself, on the pads and in life.

Your first session

No experience, no fitness test, no pressure. Here's what happens.

Step 1

Pick a class

Choose a beginner-friendly session on the timetable and let us know you're coming.

Step 2

Turn up early

Arrive ten minutes before the start in comfortable clothes with water. We'll lend you gloves and wraps.

Step 3

Train with us

A coach takes you through the basics at your own pace. You'll leave tired and wanting more.
